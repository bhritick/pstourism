Detailed Itinerary

Day-wise breakdown of your 4D/3N Sikkim journey

1 NJP / Siliguri / Bagdogra to Pelling

Pickup:

Arrival pickup from NJP Railway Station / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport.

Transfer:

Drive to Pelling in West Sikkim with beautiful Himalayan road views.

Evening:

Hotel check-in, rest and leisure around local market area.

Overnight:

Stay in Pelling.

2 West Sikkim Sightseeing

Morning:

After breakfast, start a full-day local tour in and around Pelling.

Sightseeing:

Rimbi River, Rimbi Orange Garden, Rimbi Waterfall, Kanchanjungha Waterfall, India's highest glass skywalk, Sanghak Choeling Monastery and Khecheopalri Lake.

Overnight:

Return to hotel and stay in Pelling.

3 Pelling to Ravangla via Namchi

Transfer:

After breakfast, proceed from Pelling to Ravangla via Namchi.

Visit:

Explore famous Namchi Chardham pilgrimage complex en route.

Overnight:

Reach Ravangla, hotel check-in and night stay.

4 Departure to NJP / Siliguri / Bagdogra

Morning:

Breakfast and hotel check-out.

Drop:

Drive to NJP Railway Station / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport for onward journey.

Departure:

Tour ends with memorable Sikkim holiday experiences.
