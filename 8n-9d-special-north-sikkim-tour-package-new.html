Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day 8N/9D North Sikkim route plan

1 NJP Pickup and Gangtok Transfer

Plan:

Pickup from NJP and transfer to Gangtok for overnight stay.

2 Gangtok to Dzongu

Plan:

Drive to Dzongu and experience scenic mountain landscapes and local culture.

3 Dzongu Village Sightseeing

Places:

Thingvong Village and Lingthem Village with Kanchenjunga views.

4 Dzongu to Lachen

Plan:

Scenic transfer to Lachen and overnight stay.

5 Gurudongmar and Lachung

Plan:

Visit Gurudongmar Lake, then proceed to Lachung for overnight stay.

6 Yumthang and Zero Point

Plan:

Explore Yumthang Valley and Zero Point, return to Lachung.

7 Katao and Songlaphu

Plan:

Visit Mount Katao and Songlaphu Lake with nearby hot spring points.

8 Lachung to Gangtok

Plan:

Transfer back to Gangtok and relax.

9 Departure

Plan:

Drop to NJP after breakfast.
