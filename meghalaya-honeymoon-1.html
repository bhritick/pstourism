5N/6D Meghalaya Honeymoon Package

Embark on a romantic getaway through the breathtaking landscapes of Meghalaya. From misty hills to crystal-clear rivers, explore nature’s finest creations with our carefully curated honeymoon package.

Book Now

Day 1 : Arrival in Shillong

Pickup: Guwahati Airport/Railway Station

Drive: Scenic drive to Shillong, the Scotland of the East

Stay: Overnight Stay in Shillong

Day 2 : Shillong Sightseeing

Sightseeing: Lady Hydari Park, Wards Lake, Shillong Peak, Elephant Falls

Stay: Overnight Stay in Shillong

Day 3 : Shillong to Cherrapunji

Sightseeing: Arwah Cave, Noh-Ka-Likai Falls, Eco Park, Seven Sister Falls

Stay: Overnight Stay in Cherrapunji

Day 4 : Mawlynnong & Dawki

Sightseeing: Mawlynnong Village, Single Decker Living Root Bridge, Bamboo Skywalk, Dawki Boat Ride

Stay: Overnight Stay in Cherrapunji

Day 5 : Mawphlang & Laitlum Canyon

Sightseeing: Mawphlang Sacred Forest, Khasi Heritage Village, Laitlum Canyon

Stay: Overnight Stay in Shillong

Day 6 : Departure

Drop: After breakfast, drop at Guwahati Airport/Railway Station – Trip Ends
