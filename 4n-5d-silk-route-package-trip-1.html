Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day breakdown of your Silk Route adventure

1 Arrival in Sillerygaon

  • Pickup from NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport (IXB)
  • Scenic drive to Sillerygaon, an offbeat Himalayan village
  • Free time to explore the serene surroundings and local culture
  • Enjoy panoramic views of Mt. Kanchenjunga
  • Overnight stay at Sillerygaon in comfortable accommodation

2 Sillerygaon to Rorethang/Rishikhola/Lingtam

  • After breakfast, transfer to one of the optional destinations:
    • Rorethang - Offbeat village with stunning valley views
    • Rishikhola - Peaceful riverside hamlet
    • Lingtam - Serene village amidst pine forests
  • Sightseeing en route based on chosen destination
  • Explore local villages and interact with locals
  • Overnight stay at the selected location

3 Transfer to Zuluk

  • Journey from Rishikhola/Rorethang/Lingtam to Zuluk
  • Sightseeing highlights include:
    • Kue Khola Waterfalls - Pristine cascade in the mountains
    • Aritar Lake - Serene lake surrounded by pine forests
    • Famous Zigzag Road - Marvel at the 32 hairpin bends
    • Photo stops at scenic viewpoints
  • Reach Zuluk, a small hamlet on the Old Silk Route
  • Overnight stay at Zuluk

4 Zuluk to Gangtok via Nathula Pass

  • Early morning transfer to Gangtok with extensive sightseeing:
    • Nathang Valley - Valley of flowers at 13,500 ft
    • Elephant Lake (Sela Lake) - High-altitude pristine lake
    • Changu Lake (Tsomgo Lake) - Sacred glacial lake at 12,310 ft
    • Baba Harbhajan Singh Mandir - Memorial temple with fascinating history
    • Nathula Pass - Indo-China border at 14,140 ft (subject to permit)
  • Transfer to Gangtok, capital of Sikkim
  • Evening free to explore MG Marg (Mall Road)
  • Overnight stay at Gangtok

5 Departure from Gangtok

  • After breakfast, check out from hotel
  • Optional morning sightseeing in Gangtok (if time permits)
  • Transfer to NJP Railway Station for your onward journey
  • Departure with cherished memories of the Silk Route

Important Information

  • Nathula Pass is subject to permit availability and weather conditions (closed on Mondays and Tuesdays)
  • Valid photo ID proof required for all travelers
  • Foreign nationals need special permits arranged in advance
  • Winter months (December-February) may have road closures due to snow
  • High altitude precautions necessary - carry necessary medications
  • Mobile network connectivity limited in remote areas
  • Warm clothing essential year-round due to high altitude
  • Respect local customs and environment - no littering
