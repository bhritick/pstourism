Detailed Day-Wise Itinerary

Seven-day fixed departure Andaman plan

1 Arrival at Port Blair – City Tour

Arrival:

Meet PSTourism representative at Port Blair Airport and transfer to hotel.

Sightseeing:

Cellular Jail, Netaji flag hoisting historic site, and Corbyn’s Cove Beach.

Evening:

Optional Light & Sound Show at Cellular Jail.

Stay:

Overnight in Port Blair.

2 Ross Island & North Bay Island Tour

Tour:

Two Islands tour covering Ross Island and North Bay Island (Coral Island).

Highlights:

Colonial ruins, church, bakery, cemetery, coral reefs and optional water sports.

Stay:

Return and overnight in Port Blair.

3 Port Blair to Havelock Island

Transfer:

Private premium cruise to Havelock Island and hotel check-in.

Visit:

Radhanagar Beach for swimming, sea bathing and relaxation.

Stay:

Overnight in Havelock Island.

4 Havelock Island to Neil Island

Transfer:

Cruise to Neil Island and hotel check-in.

Sightseeing:

Bharatpur Beach, Laxmanpur Beach and Natural Bridge.

Stay:

Overnight in Neil Island.

5 Neil Island to Port Blair

Optional:

Early morning Sitapur Beach sunrise for interested travellers.

Transfer:

Return by private cruise to Port Blair after breakfast.

Stay:

Overnight in Port Blair.

6 Excursion to Baratang Island

Excursion:

Early departure to Baratang via Jarwa Reserve Forest route.

Visit:

Limestone Caves through mangrove creeks (speed boat charges at own cost).

Stay:

Return and overnight in Port Blair.

7 Departure

Transfer:

After breakfast, transfer to Port Blair Airport for onward journey.

End:

Tour concludes with unforgettable Andaman memories.
