Day-wise Itinerary

Transfers, sightseeing and stays — clearly mapped

1Arrival in Jammu – Transfer to Katra

  • Arrive in Jammu; pickup and transfer to Katra.
  • Check-in at hotel; evening free for local market.
  • Overnight: Katra.

2Katra – Mata Vaishno Devi Darshan

  • Post-breakfast proceed for Vaishno Devi darshan (trek/pony/helicopter as per choice).
  • Return to Katra; rest.
  • Overnight: Katra.

3Katra – Pahalgam

  • Drive to Pahalgam (approx. 255 km / 8 hrs).
  • En-route: Jawaharlal Tunnel, saffron fields and Awantipora ruins.
  • Overnight: Pahalgam.

4Pahalgam – Srinagar

  • Morning drive to Srinagar; check-in at hotel.
  • Evening at leisure (optional: local market / lakeside time).
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

5Srinagar – Sonmarg – Srinagar

  • Full-day excursion to Sonmarg (Meadow of Gold).
  • Optional: horse ride to Thajwas Glacier / sledging (seasonal).
  • Return to Srinagar; overnight stay.

6Srinagar – Gulmarg – Srinagar

  • Excursion to Gulmarg (approx. 52 km).
  • Optional: Gondola ride (Phase 1/2 subject to availability).
  • Return to Srinagar; overnight stay.

7Doodhpathri Excursion + Houseboat Stay

  • Visit Doodhpathri (Valley of Milk) – a serene meadow escape.
  • Complimentary one-hour Shikara ride on Dal Lake.
  • Overnight: Deluxe Houseboat (Srinagar).

8Departure

  • Transfer to Srinagar Airport for departure.

Inclusions

  • All transfers & sightseeing as per itinerary
  • Accommodation in selected hotels + 1 night deluxe houseboat
  • Meals (MAP) as per package plan
  • Complimentary Shikara ride on Dal Lake
  • Driver allowance, tolls and parking (as applicable)

Exclusions

  • Air/train fare and personal expenses
  • Gondola/pony rides and adventure activities
  • Entry fees, tickets and camera fees
  • Local cab charges for restricted sightseeing zones (as per local union rules)
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions

Quick Notes

  • Prepaid SIM cards may not work in Jammu & Kashmir; postpaid connections are recommended.
  • Some local sightseeing may require union taxis as per destination rules.
  • Itinerary can be customized based on dates and hotel category.
