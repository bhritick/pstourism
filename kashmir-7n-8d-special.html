Day 1 : Arrival - Jammu to Katra

Arrival: Arrive in Jammu and transfer to Katra.

Stay: Overnight stay at the hotel in Katra.

Day 2 : Katra – Mata Vaishno Devi Darshan

Spiritual Journey: Post-breakfast trek for the spiritual darshan of Mata Vaishno Devi.

Stay: Return to the hotel for an overnight stay.

Day 3 : Katra to Pahalgam

Journey: Travel to Pahalgam, "The Valley of Shepherds" (255 km | 8 hrs).

Enroute Stops: Jawaharlal Tunnel, saffron fields, and Awantipora ruins.

Activities: Enjoy scenic views and bridle walks.

Stay: Overnight stay at the hotel in Pahalgam.

Day 4 : Pahalgam to Srinagar

Travel: Morning drive to Srinagar.

Stay: Overnight stay at the hotel in Srinagar.

Day 5 : Srinagar - Sonamarg - Srinagar

Excursion: Full-day trip to Sonamarg, "The Meadow of Gold" (166 km round trip).

Activities: Optional horse riding to Thajwas Glacier and sledging.

Stay: Return to Srinagar for an overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 6 : Srinagar to Gulmarg

Excursion: Drive to Gulmarg (52 km), famous for its ski slopes and golf course.

Activities: Optional Gondola ride to Phase 1 and Phase 2.

Stay: Return to Srinagar for an overnight stay at the hotel.

Day 7 : Srinagar to Doodhpathri - Srinagar

Excursion: Visit Doodhpathri, the serene "Valley of Milk" (100 km round trip).

Special Experience: Complimentary one-hour Shikara ride on Dal Lake.

Stay: Overnight stay in a deluxe houseboat.

Day 8 : Srinagar - Departure

Departure: Transfer to Srinagar Airport for onward journey.
