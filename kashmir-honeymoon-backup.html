Day 1 : Arrival in Jammu

Arrival: Arrive in Jammu and embark on a scenic drive to Pahalgam, "The Valley of Shepherds".

Stay: Overnight stay in Pahalgam.

Day 2 : Explore Pahalgam → Srinagar

Activities: Discover the breathtaking landscapes of Pahalgam.

Transfer: Transfer to Srinagar for an overnight stay.

Romantic Add-ons: Enjoy honeymoon cake and bed decoration.

Day 3 : Sonamarg Excursion

Full-day Trip: Enjoy a trip to Sonamarg, the "Meadow of Gold".

Activities: Exciting optional activities available (extra cost).

Stay: Return to Srinagar for overnight stay.

Day 4 : Gulmarg Excursion

Visit: Gulmarg, the "Meadow of Flowers".

Activities: Gondola rides, skiing, and more (extra cost).

Stay: Return to Srinagar for overnight stay.

Day 5 : Discover Doodhpathri

Experience: Unwind in the serene meadows and streams of Doodhpathri, the "Valley of Milk".

Stay: Return to Srinagar for overnight stay.

Day 6 : Srinagar Sightseeing

Sightseeing: Explore the Mughal Gardens Chasmesahi and more.

Special Experience: Mesmerizing Shikara Ride on Dal Lake.

Stay: Spend your last night in a luxurious houseboat.

Day 7 : Departure

Transfer: Bid farewell to Kashmir as you return to Jammu for your onward journey.
