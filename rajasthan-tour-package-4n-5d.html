Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan (timings are approximate)

1 Jaipur Sightseeing

Pickup:

Jaipur Airport (morning)

Visit:

Amer Fort (approx. 1 hr), Jal Mahal (10 min), Hawa Mahal (view on the way), City Palace + Jantar Mantar (approx. 2 hrs)

Stay:

Overnight in Jaipur

2 Jaipur to Jodhpur (Then Jaisalmer)

Transfer:

Early morning checkout and proceed to Jodhpur

Visit:

Mehrangarh Fort (30 min), Jaswant Thada (30 min), Umaid Bhawan Palace (approx. 2 hrs)

Stay:

Overnight in Jaisalmer

3 Jaisalmer Sightseeing & Desert Camp

Explore:

Jaisalmer Fort (Sonar Kella), Patwon Ki Haveli and Gadisar Lake

Desert Camp:

Check-in for cultural dance program, camel ride and jeep safari (jeep safari extra)

Stay:

Overnight at desert camp (Jaisalmer)

4 Jaisalmer to Mount Abu

Early Checkout:

Depart around 4 AM and proceed to Mount Abu

Visit:

Dilwara Jain Temple, Om Shanti Bhawan and Nakki Lake (evening)

Stay:

Overnight in Mount Abu

5 Departure

Transfer:

Checkout and transfer to Jaipur Airport (approx. 12 hours drive)

Drop:

Airport drop-off
