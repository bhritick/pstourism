Day 1 : Arrival in Leh – Acclimatization & Relaxation

Arrival: Arrive at Leh Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport, one of the highest airports in the world.

Activities: Rest for acclimatization to Ladakh’s high altitude.

Stay: Overnight stay in Leh.

Day 2 : Leh Local Sightseeing – Discover the Heart of Ladakh

Sightseeing: Visit Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Spituk Monastery, Leh Palace, Shey Palace, Shanti Stupa & Leh Market.

Stay: Overnight stay in Leh.

Day 3 : Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La – The Highest Motorable Pass

Experience: Drive through Khardung La Pass (18,380 ft), one of the world’s highest motorable roads.

Activities: Explore Hundar sand dunes and enjoy a Bactrian camel safari (optional).

Stay: Overnight stay in Hundar / Deskit.

Day 4 : Nubra Valley to Pangong Tso – Witness Ladakh’s Jewel

Visit: Pangong Lake, featured in the Bollywood movie 3 Idiots.

Experience: Mesmerizing blue waters and changing colors of the lake.

Stay: Overnight stay in Pangong Tent / Camp.

Day 5 : Pangong to Tso Moriri – Explore the Hidden Gem of Ladakh

Travel: Scenic drive to Tso Moriri Lake, another high-altitude beauty.

Stay: Overnight stay in Tso Moriri.

Day 6 : Tso Moriri to Leh – Scenic Return Journey

Drive: Return to Leh, enjoying picturesque views along the way.

Stay: Overnight stay in Leh.

Day 7 : Departure from Leh – Farewell Ladakh!

Transfer: After breakfast, transfer to Leh Airport for your return journey.
