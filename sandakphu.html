Sandakphu Trek Itineraries

Classic Sandakphu-Phalut Trek

6 Days / 5 Nights

  • Route: Manebhanjan → Tonglu → Kalipokhri → Sandakphu → Phalut → Srikhola
  • Highlights: Sunrise over Kanchenjunga, Phalut's 360° views
  • Group Size: 8-12 people
  • Best Time: March-June, Oct-Dec
  • Difficulty: Moderate
Most Popular

Winter Sandakphu Trek

5 Days / 4 Nights

  • Route: Manebhanjan → Tonglu → Sandakphu → Gurdum → Rimbik
  • Highlights: Snow-covered trails, pristine mountain views
  • Group Size: 6-10 people
  • Best Time: December-February
  • Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging
Winter Special

Quick Sandakphu Trek

4 Days / 3 Nights

  • Route: Manebhanjan → Tumling → Sandakphu → Gurdum
  • Highlights: Quick summit, Kanchenjunga views
  • Group Size: 6-10 people
  • Best Time: March-May, Oct-Nov
  • Difficulty: Easy to Moderate
Weekend Trek

Extended Singalila Ridge

8 Days / 7 Nights

  • Route: Full Singalila Ridge exploration including remote villages
  • Highlights: Complete ridge walk, wildlife spotting, cultural immersion
  • Group Size: 4-8 people
  • Best Time: April-May, Oct-Nov
  • Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging
