Discover Kalimpong Beyond the Ordinary

When travelers think of North Bengal, Darjeeling often steals the spotlight. But tucked away in the hills lies Kalimpong—a peaceful, scenic destination teeming with nature, history, culture, and unexplored gems. This Offbeat Kalimpong Package Tour is designed for those seeking serenity, adventure, and an authentic North Bengal experience.

From Kolakham sightseeing to Kafergaon stay, from Ramdhura viewpoints to the Lava Monastery visit, our carefully curated 4 nights 5 days Kalimpong itinerary ensures you experience the essence of offbeat Himalayan travel.

4 Nights 5 Days Kalimpong Itinerary

An Offbeat Journey Through Hidden North Bengal

1

Day 1: Pickup and Kolakham Sightseeing

  • Pickup from NJP / Bagdogra / Siliguri
  • Scenic drive via Gajoldoba (if time permits), Gorubathan, Ambiok Tea Garden, Paparkheti, and Chell River
  • Enjoy glimpses of the stunning pine forests Kalimpong is known for
  • Reach Kolakham and check-in at a beautiful Kalimpong homestay
  • Overnight stay: Kolakham
2

Day 2: Kolakham to Kafergaon via Lava, Lolegaon & Changey Falls

  • After breakfast, head towards Kafergaon
  • Visit:
    • Changey Falls – the tallest waterfall in the region
    • Lava Monastery – soak in the peace of Buddhist chants
    • Lava Local Market – shop local handicrafts
    • Lolegaon Canopy Walk – walk among the treetops
  • The scenic drive is a treat with lush pine forests lining your path
  • Overnight stay: Kafergaon
3

Day 3: Kafergaon to Kagey via Rishop & Nokdara Lake

  • Post breakfast, start for Kagey village
  • En route, explore:
    • Rishop – a peaceful, panoramic hamlet with Kanchenjunga views
    • Nokdara Lake – quiet lake surrounded by hills
  • Evening: Enjoy a bonfire or BBQ at your own cost
  • Overnight stay: Kagey
4

Day 4: Kagey to Ramdhura via Delo Park

  • After breakfast, head to Ramdhura
  • Visit:
    • Delo Park – flower gardens and paragliding
    • Hanuman Tok – stunning views and spiritual vibe
    • Science Centre – for families and curious minds
    • Ramdhura viewpoint – stunning Teesta River valley views
  • Overnight stay: Ramdhura
5

Day 5: Departure via Jalsa Bungalow & Sevoke Teesta View

  • After breakfast, begin your return journey
  • On the way, explore:
    • Jalsa Bungalow – colonial mansion in greenery
    • Jalsa View Point
    • Sevoke Teesta view – iconic frame of the mighty Teesta
  • Tour ends with unforgettable offbeat memories!
