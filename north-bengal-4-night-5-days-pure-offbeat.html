Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan for your offbeat Kalimpong tour

1 Pickup and Kolakham Sightseeing

Pickup:

NJP / Bagdogra / Siliguri

Route:

Scenic drive via Gajoldoba (if time permits), Gorubathan, Ambiok Tea Garden, Paparkheti and Chell River

Experience:

Glimpses of Kalimpong’s pine forests

Stay:

Kolakham (homestay)

2 Kolakham to Kafergaon via Lava, Lolegaon & Changey Falls

After Breakfast:

Head towards Kafergaon

Visit:

Changey Falls, Lava Monastery, Lava Local Market, Lolegaon Canopy Walk

Drive:

Scenic pine-forest route throughout the day

Stay:

Kafergaon (homestay)

3 Kafergaon to Kagey via Rishop & Nokdara Lake

Post Breakfast:

Start for Kagey village

En Route:

Rishop (panoramic hamlet with Kanchenjunga views), Nokdara Lake

Evening (Optional):

Bonfire / BBQ at your own cost

Stay:

Kagey

4 Kagey to Ramdhura via Delo Park

After Breakfast:

Head to Ramdhura

Visit:

Delo Park, Hanuman Tok, Science Centre, Ramdhura viewpoint (Teesta Valley views)

Stay:

Ramdhura

5 Departure via Jalsa Bungalow & Sevoke Teesta View

After Breakfast:

Begin your return journey

Stops:

Jalsa Bungalow, Jalsa View Point, Sevoke Teesta view

Drop:

NJP / IXB / Siliguri (as per booking)
