Romantic 6-Day North Bengal & Sikkim Honeymoon Tour

Experience the perfect blend of romance and adventure with our exclusive 5N/6D North Bengal & East Sikkim Honeymoon Package. From the breathtaking landscapes of Gangtok and Darjeeling to the tranquil beauty of Sittong, this romantic getaway is designed for couples seeking unforgettable memories amidst the Himalayas.

Book Now

Honeymoon Package Itinerary

Day 1 : Arrival in Gangtok – The Gateway to the Himalayas

Private Pick-up: From Bagdogra Airport/NJP Railway Station

Check-in: At your deluxe hotel in Gangtok

Leisure Time: Relax and explore local markets

Stay: Overnight stay in Gangtok

Day 2 : Gangtok Sightseeing

Visit: Changu Lake (Tsomgo Lake) – A mesmerizing glacial lake

Explore: Baba Mandir (New) – A sacred destination in East Sikkim

Optional: Nathu-La Pass – A high-altitude border pass with stunning views

Stay: Overnight stay in Gangtok

Day 3 : Gangtok to Darjeeling – The Queen of the Hills

Drive: To Darjeeling, the land of tea gardens

Check-in: At your deluxe hotel in Darjeeling

Leisure Time: Explore local markets and enjoy the evening

Stay: Overnight stay in Darjeeling

Day 4 : Darjeeling Sightseeing

Visit: Tiger Hill – Witness the magical sunrise over Kanchenjunga Batashia Loop, Ghoom Monastery, Peace Pagoda, Japanese Temple, Tenzing Rock, Rangeet Valley Tea Gaurden,HMI , Zoo , Museum, Ropeway

Stay: Overnight stay in Darjeeling

Day 5 : Darjeeling to Sittong – A Hidden Himalayan Gem

Drive: To Sittong, a serene village surrounded by nature

Visit: Lamahatta Eco Park, Tukdah & Tinchuley Viewpoints, Lovers meet point, Peasak tea garden

Stay: Overnight stay in Sittong

Day 6 : Departure – Sittong to Bagdogra/NJP

Transfer: To Bagdogra Airport/NJP Railway Station after breakfast With sightseeing of Namching Pokri , Mahaldhiram Tea garden , Ahaldhara view points

Farewell: Bid farewell to the enchanting beauty of North Bengal & Sikkim
