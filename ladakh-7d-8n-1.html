Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan (7 Nights / 8 Days)

1 Arrival at Srinagar

Plan:

Acclimatization, leisure, final presentations and rest.

Stay:

Overnight stay in Srinagar

2 Srinagar to Kargil via Sonmarg

Drive & visit:

Drive via Sonmarg; visit Namika-la, Lamayuru monastery, Kargil War Museum and Zoji'la Pass as per route/time.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Kargil

3 Kargil to Leh

Route:

Drive to Shey/Leh; visit Lamayuru monastery, Alchi monastery and Drass as per route/time.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Shey/Leh

4 Leh Local Sightseeing

Explore:

Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Leh Palace, Shey Palace, Shanti Stupa and Leh Market.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Shey/Leh

5 Shey/Leh to Hundar via Khardungla Pass

Experience:

Drive to Nubra Valley via Khardungla Pass.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Hunder/Deskit

6 Hunder/Deskit to Pangong Tso

Visit:

Drive to Pangong Lake; visit 3 Idiots Point.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Pangong camp

7 Pangong Tso to Shey/Leh via Chang-la Pass

En-route:

Chang-la Pass and Changla Baba Temple.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Shey/Leh

8 Departure from Shey/Leh

Transfer:

Drop at the airport. End of trip.
