Essential Travel Tips for Leh Ladakh

Make your journey safe and memorable

Acclimatize Properly

Spend at least 48 hours in Leh before heading to higher altitudes. Avoid alcohol and heavy exercise initially.

Pack Smart

  • Warm layers (even in summer)
  • Sunglasses & sunscreen
  • Medications for altitude sickness
  • Power bank (limited electricity)

Permits & Documents

Indian Nationals: Inner Line Permit (ILP) required for certain areas.

Foreigners: Protected Area Permit (PAP) needed.

All: Carry enough cash (ATMs are scarce).
