Tour Itinerary

5 Days of Scenic Beauty & Cultural Exploration

1

Day 1: Arrival & Kalimpong Homestay Experience

  • Arrival at NJP Station/Bagdogra Airport
  • Private transfer to Kalimpong
  • Check into a serene homestay nestled in pine forests
  • Meals: Dinner at the homestay (AP Plan)
Highlights: Kalimpong homestay experience Scenic drive through North Bengal
2

Day 2: Kalimpong Sightseeing

  • Morning: Visit Delo Park (panoramic Himalayan views)
  • Explore Morgan House (colonial-era heritage)
  • Discover Durpin Monastery & Cactus Garden
  • Sunset at Hanuman Top
  • Meals: Breakfast & dinner at the homestay
Highlights: Delo Park Kalimpong Morgan House Kalimpong
3

Day 3: Transfer to Darjeeling

  • After breakfast, depart for Darjeeling
  • Stop at:
    • Gumbadhara Viewpoint (360° Himalayan vistas)
    • Lover's Meet Point (Teesta & Rangeet rivers)
    • Lamahata Eco Park (nature trails)
    • Tinchule Viewpoint (stunning sunset spot)
  • Meals: Breakfast & dinner in Darjeeling
Highlights: Lamahata Eco Park tour Tinchule Viewpoint Darjeeling
4

Day 4: Leisure Day in Darjeeling

  • Free time to explore at your own pace
  • Stroll along Mall Road, visit local cafés
  • Optional: Toy Train ride (Darjeeling Himalayan Railway)
  • Meals: Breakfast & dinner
Highlights: Darjeeling's old-world charm
5

Day 5: Darjeeling Highlights & Departure

  • Morning:
    • Batasia Loop (iconic toy train route)
    • Ghoom Monastery (oldest Tibetan Buddhist site)
    • Peace Pagoda (spiritual serenity)
    • Darjeeling Zoo (Himalayan wildlife)
  • Afternoon:
    • Rangeet Valley Tea Garden (tea-tasting)
    • Drive to Mirik Lake (serene boating)
  • Drop-off at NJP/Bagdogra
Highlights: Batasia Loop Darjeeling Mirik Lake day trip
