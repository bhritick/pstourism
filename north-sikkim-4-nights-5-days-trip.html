North Sikkim Tour Itinerary

4N/5D or 5N/6D Himalayan Adventure (NJP-NJP)

1

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer to Gangtok

  • Pickup from NJP/Siliguri/IXB / Gangtok stand
  • Scenic drive to Gangtok, the capital of Sikkim
  • Overnight Stay: Gangtok
2

Day 2: Gangtok to Lachen

  • See beautiful waterfalls enroute
  • Lachen - picturesque Himalayan village
  • Overnight Stay: Lachen
3

Day 3: Gurudongmar Lake & Transfer to Lachung

  • 17,800 ft Visit Gurudongmar Lake (one of highest lakes in world)
  • Transfer to Lachung village
  • Overnight Stay: Lachung
4

Day 4: Yumthang Valley & Return to Gangtok

  • Explore Yumthang Valley (Valley of Flowers)
  • Optional: Zero Point (extra cost)
  • Overnight Stay: Gangtok
5

Day 5: Departure OR Extended Exploration

  • 4N/5D Package: Drop at NJP/Siliguri/IXB
  • 5N/6D Package: Explore Tsomgo Lake, Baba Mandir, Nathula Pass
6

Day 6: Departure (5N/6D Package)

  • After breakfast, drop at NJP/Siliguri/IXB
  • Tour concludes with unforgettable memories
Note: Itinerary subject to change based on weather, road conditions and permit availability.
