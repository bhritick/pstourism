Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day breakdown of your romantic Darjeeling honeymoon

1 Arrival via Mirik – Welcome to Paradise

Pickup:

From NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport

Activities:

Scenic drive via Mirik – A hidden gem of North Bengal

Highlights:

• Romantic boat ride at Mirik Lake
• Explore Pashupati Market (Nepal Border)
• Stop at Simana Viewpoint for mesmerizing Indo-Nepal panoramic view

Stay:

Check-in at your honeymoon-special 2-star hotel in Darjeeling
Evening leisure time at Mall Road
Candlelight dinner and overnight stay in Darjeeling

2 Darjeeling Full-Day Sightseeing – Love in the Hills

Morning:

Early morning sunrise view from Tiger Hill (Perfect for couples!) 🌄

Sightseeing:

• Visit Batasia Loop & Ghoom Monastery for a peaceful and serene experience

Afternoon:

• Visit Japanese Temple & Peace Pagoda
• Experience the Darjeeling Ropeway (Cable Car Ride)
• Explore Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI) & Museum

Evening:

• Visit Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park (Darjeeling Zoo)
• Tenzing Rock
• Rangeet Valley Tea Garden
• Relaxed romantic evening at your hotel with special dinner for the couple
• Overnight stay in Darjeeling

3 Darjeeling to Sittong – The Hidden Romance

Check-out:

From Darjeeling

Highlights:

• Visit Lovers' Meet Point
• Stroll through Peasok Tea Garden
• Explore Lamahata Eco Park
• Scenic drive through Tukdah & Tinchuley Viewpoints

Stay:

Check-in at your charming Sittong homestay
Explore orange orchards & serene nature trails
Traditional candlelit dinner at Sittong homestay
Overnight stay in Sittong

4 Sittong to NJP Drop – Lasting Memories

Check-out:

From Sittong

Highlights:

• Visit Namthing Pokhri
• Stop at Ahaldara Viewpoint
• Explore Mahaldhiram Tea Garden

Departure:

Transfer to NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport for departure
