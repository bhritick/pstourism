3N/4D Darjeeling Offbeat Honeymoon Package | PSTourism

Experience a magical honeymoon in Darjeeling, blended with offbeat charm and breathtaking natural beauty. From misty hills and lush tea gardens to romantic sunset points and serene monasteries, this 3N/4D honeymoon package is designed for couples seeking intimacy, adventure, and unforgettable memories.

Book Now

Day 1 : Arrival via Mirik – Welcome to Paradise

Pickup: From NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport.

Activities: Scenic drive via Mirik – A hidden gem of North Bengal.

Highlights: Romantic boat ride at Mirik Lake, explore Pashupati Market (Nepal Border), and stop at Simana Viewpoint for a mesmerizing Indo-Nepal panoramic view.

Stay: Check-in at your honeymoon-special 2-star hotel in Darjeeling. Evening leisure time at Mall Road. Candlelight dinner and overnight stay in Darjeeling.

Day 2 : Darjeeling Full-Day Sightseeing – Love in the Hills

Morning: Early morning sunrise view from Tiger Hill (Perfect for couples!) 🌄.

Sightseeing: Visit Batasia Loop & Ghoom Monastery for a peaceful and serene experience.

Afternoon: Visit Japanese Temple & Peace Pagoda, experience the Darjeeling Ropeway (Cable Car Ride), and explore Himalayan Mountaineering Institute (HMI) & Museum.

Evening: Visit Padmaja Naidu Himalayan Zoological Park (Darjeeling Zoo), Tenzing Rock, and Rangeet Valley Tea Garden. Enjoy a relaxed romantic evening at your hotel with a special dinner for the couple. Overnight stay in Darjeeling.

Day 3 : Darjeeling to Sittong – The Hidden Romance

Check-out: From Darjeeling.

Highlights: Visit Lovers’ Meet Point, stroll through Peasok Tea Garden, and explore Lamahata Eco Park. Scenic drive through Tukdah & Tinchuley Viewpoints.

Stay: Check-in at your charming Sittong homestay. Explore orange orchards & serene nature trails. Traditional candlelit dinner at Sittong homestay. Overnight stay in Sittong.

Day 4 : Sittong to NJP Drop – Lasting Memories

Check-out: From Sittong.

Highlights: Visit Namthing Pokhri, stop at Ahaldara Viewpoint, and explore Mahaldhiram Tea Garden.

Departure: Transfer to NJP Railway Station / Bagdogra Airport for departure.
