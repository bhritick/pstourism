Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan for the circuit

1 Deoghar (Baba Baidyanath Dham)

Pickup:

Jasidih Station pickup and transfer to Deoghar

Darshan:

Baba Baidyanath Dham; puja support with a local panda (optional)

Sightseeing:

Trikut Hill, Satsang Ashram, Nolokha Mandir, Nandan Pahar

Stay:

Overnight in Deoghar

2 Deoghar to Rajgir (Pavapuri • Nalanda • Kundalpur)

Travel:

Deoghar to Bakhtiyarpur (train/cab as per plan) and proceed to Rajgir

En-route:

Sightseeing of Pavapuri, Nalanda and Kundalpur

Stay:

Overnight in Rajgir

3 Rajgir Local Sightseeing

Visit:

Pandu Pokhar, Vishwa Shanti Stupa, Venuvana, Japanese Stupa

More:

Vulture’s Peak (Griddhakuta), Makhdum Kund, Bimbisara’s Jail

Stay:

Overnight in Rajgir

4 Bodh Gaya & Gaya Sightseeing (Drop)

Bodh Gaya:

Mahabodhi Temple, Bodhi Tree, Great Buddha Statue, Thai Monastery

Also:

Cankamana and Muchakinda Lake

Drop:

Drop at Gaya Station after sightseeing
