Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan for your Bhutan holiday

1 Jaigaon/Phuentsholing to Thimphu

Arrival:

Arrive at Jaigaon/Phuentsholing and begin the journey

Drive:

Scenic transfer to Thimphu with en-route sightseeing

Stay:

Overnight in Thimphu

2 Thimphu to Punakha (Dochu-La) & back

Route:

Drive to Punakha via Dochu-La Pass

Visit:

Punakha Dzong and Suspension Bridge

Optional:

River rafting (extra cost)

Stay:

Return and overnight in Thimphu

3 Thimphu Sightseeing & transfer to Paro

Highlights:

Buddha Point, BBS Tower, Takin Zoo, Simply Bhutan Museum, Clock Tower Square, Kaja Throm

Evening:

Transfer to Paro

Stay:

Overnight in Paro

4 Paro Local Sightseeing

Start:

Paro town and International Airport viewpoint

Visit:

Tiger’s Nest Monastery (trek), Nya Mey Zam Bridge, Zinpung Dzong, Kichu Lakhang

Stay:

Overnight in Paro

5 Paro to Phuentsholing/Jaigaon Drop

Departure:

After breakfast, drive back to Phuentsholing/Jaigaon

Drop:

Tour ends with wonderful memories of Bhutan
