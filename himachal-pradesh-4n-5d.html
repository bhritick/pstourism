Shimla-Manali Tour Itinerary

4 Nights / 5 Days Himalayan Escape

1

Day 1: Arrival in Shimla

  • Pickup from Shimla Volvo Bus Stand
  • Transfer to hotel and sightseeing:
  • Kufri, Green Valley
  • Sankat Mochan Temple, Jakhoo Temple
  • Shimla Mall Road
  • Optional: Tara Devi Temple (extra cost)
  • Overnight Stay: Shimla
2

Day 2: Shimla to Manali Transfer

  • Scenic drive via:
  • Sundernagar Lake
  • Pandoh Dam
  • Overnight Stay: Manali
3

Day 3: Manali Local Sightseeing

  • Hadimba Devi Temple
  • Vashisht Temple (Hot Water Springs)
  • Manu Temple (walk only)
  • Club House
  • Van Vihar
  • Tibetan Monastery
  • Overnight Stay: Manali
4

Day 4: Solang Valley & Rohtang

  • Solang Valley
  • Atal Tunnel
  • Rohtang Pass (if open, extra cost)
  • Overnight Stay: Manali
5

Day 5: Departure

  • Drop at Manali Private Volvo Bus Stand
  • Tour ends with sweet memories
Note: Itinerary may change based on weather and road conditions.
