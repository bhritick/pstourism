Day-wise Itinerary

Simple, romantic and comfortable pacing

1Arrival – Jammu to Pahalgam

  • Arrive in Jammu; pickup and proceed to Pahalgam.
  • Evening at leisure for riverside walks.
  • Overnight: Pahalgam.

2Explore Pahalgam → Srinagar

  • Explore Pahalgam (local cab may be required for select valleys).
  • Drive to Srinagar; check-in.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

3Sonmarg Excursion

  • Full-day trip to Sonmarg (Meadow of Gold).
  • Optional: horse ride to Thajwas Glacier (seasonal).
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

4Gulmarg Excursion

  • Visit Gulmarg; enjoy the meadows and viewpoints.
  • Optional: Gondola ride / snow activities (seasonal).
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

5Doodhpathri Day Trip

  • Relax in Doodhpathri (Valley of Milk) — a quiet meadow escape.
  • Return to Srinagar.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

6Srinagar Sightseeing + Houseboat Night

  • Local sightseeing (Mughal Gardens / markets as per time).
  • Shikara ride on Dal Lake.
  • Overnight: Houseboat (Srinagar).

7Departure

  • Departure transfer as per plan (Jammu/Srinagar).

What’s Included

  • Meals: breakfast & dinner (as per package plan) + taxes
  • Transport: AC Etios/Dzire (as per package)
  • Honeymoon add-ons: cake, candlelight dinner, bed decoration
  • Dal Lake Shikara ride
  • All transfers & sightseeing as per itinerary

Exclusions

  • Adventure activities (Gondola/pony rides, etc.)
  • Personal expenses
  • Entry fees / tickets (if applicable)
