Day 1 : Arrival in Shimla – The Beginning of Love

Pickup: From Chandigarh / Shimla Railway Station and transfer to a cozy hotel in Shimla.

Activities: Stroll through Shimla Mall Road (walking only).

Stay: Overnight stay in Shimla.

Day 2 : Shimla Local Sightseeing – Explore Love Amidst Nature

Sightseeing: Visit Taradevi Temple (cost extra), Kufri, Green Valley, Sankat Mochan Temple, Jakhoo Temple, and Shimla Mall Road.

Dinner: Enjoy a candlelight dinner with breathtaking views.

Stay: Overnight stay in Shimla.

Day 3 : Shimla to Manikaran / Kasol – A Scenic Drive

Travel: Transfer to Manikaran / Kasol, a paradise for honeymooners.

En Route: Visit Sundernagar Lake and Pandoh Dam.

Stay: Overnight stay in Manikaran / Kasol.

Day 4 : Manikaran & Kasol – Serenity and Spiritual Bliss

Visit: Manikaran Gurudwara, Parvati River, Natural Hot Springs, and Kasol Market.

Activities: Riverside walk and capturing special moments.

Stay: Overnight stay in Manikaran / Kasol.

Day 5 : Manikaran / Kasol to Manali – The Queen of Hills

Travel: Transfer to Manali with a romantic stop at Kullu.

Activities: River Rafting, Paragliding (cost extra), or explore Kullu market.

Stay: Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 6 : Manali Local Sightseeing – A Day of Romance & Adventure

Sightseeing: Visit Vashisht Temple, Hidimba Devi Temple, Club House, Manu Temple (walking only), Van Vihar, and Tibetan Monastery.

Activities: Enjoy quality time at a cozy café or explore the town hand in hand.

Stay: Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 7 : Manali Excursion – A Snowy Affair

Visit: Atal Tunnel & Rohtang Pass (if open, cost extra) & Solang Valley.

Activities: Skiing, snowboarding, or a romantic horse ride (optional).

Stay: Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 8 : Departure – Taking Memories Back Home

Transfer: After breakfast, transfer to Kalka / Chandigarh Railway Station for departure.
