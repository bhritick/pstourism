Day-wise Itinerary

Planned for smooth transfers and sightseeing

1Arrival in Srinagar + Local Sightseeing

  • Pickup from Srinagar Airport; transfer to hotel.
  • Mughal Gardens: Cheshmashahi, Nishat Bagh, Shalimar Bagh.
  • Evening Shikara ride on Dal Lake; local market visit.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

2Srinagar – Sonmarg – Srinagar

  • Full-day excursion to Sonmarg (Meadow of Gold).
  • Optional: horse ride to Thajwas Glacier / sledge rides (seasonal).
  • Return to Srinagar; overnight stay.

3Srinagar – Gulmarg – Srinagar

  • Excursion to Gulmarg.
  • Optional: Gondola ride / Khilanmarg visit (as per availability).
  • Return to Srinagar; overnight stay.

4Srinagar – Pahalgam

  • Drive to Pahalgam via saffron fields and Awantipora ruins.
  • Afternoon at leisure.
  • Note: local cab required for Aru Valley, Betaab Valley & Chandanwari.
  • Overnight: Pahalgam.

5Pahalgam – Srinagar (Houseboat)

  • Optional: Baisaran (Mini Switzerland) via pony ride.
  • Return to Srinagar; check-in to deluxe houseboat.
  • Overnight: Srinagar (Houseboat).

6Departure

  • Drop-off at Srinagar Airport.

Inclusions

  • All transfers & sightseeing as per itinerary
  • Hotel + 1 night deluxe houseboat stay
  • Meals (MAP) as per package plan
  • Dal Lake Shikara ride
  • Driver allowance, tolls and parking (as applicable)

Exclusions

  • Gondola/pony rides and any adventure activities
  • Personal expenses, tips and meals not mentioned
  • Local cab charges in Pahalgam (Aru/Betaab/Chandanwari)
  • Entry fees, tickets and camera fees
  • Anything not mentioned in inclusions
