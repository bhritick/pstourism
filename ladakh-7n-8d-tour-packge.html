Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan for your 7N/8D Ladakh trip

1 Arrival in Srinagar

Welcome:

Arrive in Srinagar, the summer capital of Jammu & Kashmir.

Activities:

Leisure day for relaxation and trip briefing.

Stay:

Overnight in Srinagar

2 Srinagar → Kargil (via Sonmarg)

Route:

Travel through Sonmarg; pass scenic mountain stretches en-route.

Sightseeing:

Visit key points like Kargil War Museum (time permitting).

Stay:

Overnight in Kargil

3 Kargil → Leh

Visit:

Drive towards Leh; explore culture and landscapes en-route (route/time dependent).

Stay:

Overnight in Leh

4 Leh Local Sightseeing

Sightseeing:

Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Leh Palace, Shey Palace, Shanti Stupa & Leh Market.

Stay:

Overnight in Leh

5 Leh → Nubra Valley (Hunder/Deskit) via Khardungla

Experience:

Drive via Khardungla Pass—one of the highest motorable roads.

Stay:

Overnight at Hunder/Deskit

6 Nubra → Pangong Tso

Visit:

Pangong Lake—famous for the 3 Idiots shooting spot.

Stay:

Overnight at Pangong tent/camp

7 Pangong → Leh via Changla

Drive:

Return to Leh via Changla Pass; visit Changla Baba Temple.

Stay:

Overnight in Leh

8 Departure

Transfer:

Airport drop for your onward journey.
