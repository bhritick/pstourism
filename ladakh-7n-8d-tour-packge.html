Day 1 : Arrival in Srinagar – The Gateway to Ladakh

Welcome: Arrive in Srinagar, the summer capital of Jammu & Kashmir.

Activities: Leisure day for acclimatization, relaxation, and trip briefing.

Stay: Overnight stay in Srinagar.

Day 2 : Srinagar to Kargil via Sonmarg – Scenic Drive to Ladakh

Route: Travel through Sonmarg, Namika La Pass, Lamayuru Monastery.

Sightseeing: Visit Kargil War Museum, Minimarg Zoji La, Kangaroo Curves.

Stay: Overnight stay in Kargil.

Day 3 : Kargil to Leh – Journey Through Ancient Ladakh

Visit: Drass, Alchi Monastery, and 1000-year-old fresco paintings.

Explore: Local culture and scenic landscapes.

Stay: Overnight stay in Leh.

Day 4 : Leh Local Sightseeing – Discover the Heart of Ladakh

Sightseeing: Visit Gurudwara Pathar Sahib, Leh Palace, Shey Palace, Shanti Stupa & Leh Market.

Stay: Overnight stay in Leh.

Day 5 : Leh to Hundar via Khardung La Pass – Highest Motorable Road

Experience: Khardung La Pass – one of the highest roads in the world.

Stay: Overnight stay in Hundar / Deskit.

Day 6 : Hunder / Deskit to Pangong Tso – Iconic Ladakh Lake Tour

Visit: Pangong Lake, famous for the 3 Idiots movie shooting spot.

Experience: Serenity of the high-altitude lake.

Stay: Overnight stay in Pangong Tent / Camp.

Day 7 : Pangong Tso to Leh via Chang La Pass – Breathtaking Views

Drive: Through Chang La Pass and visit Changla Baba Temple.

Stay: Overnight stay in Leh.

Day 8 : Departure from Leh – Goodbye Ladakh!

Transfer: To Leh Airport for your return journey.
