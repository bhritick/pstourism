Day 1 : Arrival in Shillong

Pickup: Arrive in Shillong and check into your deluxe accommodation.

Stay: Overnight stay in Shillong.

Day 2 : Shillong Sightseeing

Sightseeing: Lady Hydari Park, Wards Lake, Shillong Peak, and Elephant Falls.

Stay: Overnight stay in Shillong.

Day 3 : Cherrapunji Exploration

Sightseeing: Noh-Ka-Likai Falls, Eco Park, Seven Sister Falls, and Mawsmai Cave.

Stay: Overnight stay in Cherrapunji.

Day 4 : Mawlynnong & Dawki

Sightseeing: Mawlynnong Village, Single Decker Living Root Bridge, Dawki Village, and Umngot River boat ride.

Stay: Overnight stay in Cherrapunji.

Day 5 : Mawphlang & Laitlum

Sightseeing: Mawphlang Sacred Forest, Khasi Heritage Village, and Laitlum Canyon.

Stay: Overnight stay in Shillong.

Day 6 : Departure

Drop: After breakfast, drop at Shillong for onward travel.
