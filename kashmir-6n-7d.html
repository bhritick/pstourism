Day-wise Itinerary

Heavenly Kashmir Explorer – 7 day plan

1Arrival at Jammu | Transfer to Srinagar

  • Meet our representative at Jammu Railway Station / Airport.
  • Scenic transfer to Srinagar (approx. 300 km | 9–10 hrs).
  • Hotel check-in and rest.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

2Srinagar Local Sightseeing

  • Visit Shankaracharya Temple for panoramic city and Dal Lake views.
  • Explore Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh.
  • Visit carpet weaving unit, walnut wood carving and papier-mâché craft demos.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

3Srinagar – Sonamarg – Srinagar

  • Full-day excursion to Sonamarg (Meadow of Gold) in Sindh Valley.
  • Optional: pony ride to Thajiwas Glacier and snow sledging (own cost).
  • Return to Srinagar by evening.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

4Srinagar – Gulmarg – Srinagar

  • Drive to Gulmarg (Meadow of Flowers), approx. 2,730 m altitude.
  • Optional: Gondola Cable Car (Phase 1 & 2), pony rides.
  • Return to Srinagar by evening.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

5Srinagar – Pahalgam

  • Drive to Pahalgam (approx. 95 km | 3 hrs).
  • En route: saffron fields, Awantipora ruins, countryside views.
  • Leisure walk in Pahalgam valley.
  • Overnight: Pahalgam.

6Pahalgam – Srinagar

  • Optional sightseeing (own cost): Aru Valley, Chandanwari, Baisaran by pony.
  • Later drive back to Srinagar.
  • Check in to hotel / houseboat.
  • Overnight: Srinagar.

7Srinagar to Jammu | Departure

  • After breakfast, transfer to Jammu for onward journey.
  • Tour ends with beautiful memories of Kashmir.
