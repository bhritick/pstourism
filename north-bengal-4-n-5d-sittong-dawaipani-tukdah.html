Why Choose an Offbeat North Bengal Tour?

This region of Bengal holds many unexplored gems—lush tea gardens, scenic viewpoints, cultural hamlets, and eco-tourism spots.

From Sittong's orange orchards to Takdah's colonial charm, Dawaipani's mountain silence to Mirik's panoramic beauty, this 4 nights 5 days North Bengal trip brings you the best offbeat destinations near Darjeeling. Whether you're a solo traveler, a nature lover, or a couple seeking serenity, this itinerary is perfect for you.

4 Nights 5 Days North Bengal Itinerary

An Offbeat Journey Through Hidden Himalayan Destinations

1

Day 1: Arrival at Sittong – The Soul of North Bengal

  • Transfer from NJP/Bagdogra Airport to Sittong
  • Sightseeing at:
    • Namthing Pokhri – Seasonal lake with salamanders
    • Mahaldiram Tea Garden – Panoramic tea estate views
    • Ahaldhara Viewpoint – Magical sunrise point
    • Yogighat Bridge – Ancient hanging bridge
    • Rabindra Museum – Tagore's stay in Mongpoo
  • Accommodation: Homestay at Sittong
2

Day 2: Journey to Dawaipani – The Hidden Hamlet

  • After breakfast, drive to Dawaipani
  • En route attractions:
    • Lover's Meet View Point – Teesta-Rangeet confluence
    • Pesak Tea Garden – Scenic tea estate
  • Accommodation: Traditional Homestay in Dawaipani
3

Day 3: Scenic Ride to Takdah – Offbeat Colonial Charm

  • Head towards Takdah through forested trails
  • Sightseeing:
    • Gumbadhara View Point – Peaceful mountain views
    • Takdah Tinchule View Point – 360° Himalayan vistas
  • Accommodation: Vintage colonial homestay in Takdah
4

Day 4: Takdah to Darjeeling via Lamahatta Eco Park

  • Travel through pine forests to Darjeeling
  • Activities:
    • Lamahatta Eco Park – Serene picnic spot
    • Darjeeling Mall Road – Cafes, shops & street food
  • Accommodation: Stay in Darjeeling town
5

Day 5: Return via Mirik – End of the Journey

  • After breakfast, begin return journey
  • Places to visit:
    • Mirik Lake – Tranquil pine-surrounded lake
    • Pasupati Market – Bustling Nepal border market
    • Simana Viewpoint – Darjeeling-Nepal ridge views
    • Bokar Monastery – Peaceful monastery
    • Gopal Dhara Tea Garden – Famous Darjeeling tea
  • Drop-off at NJP/IXB/SLG with unforgettable memories
