About PSTourism

ISO 9001:2015 Certified Travel Company | Delivering Quality & Affordable Travel Experiences Since 2020

Why Choose PSTourism?

At PSTourism (Pather Sandhane Tourism), we are dedicated to delivering exceptional travel experiences with a strong focus on quality, affordability, and safety. As an ISO 9001:2015 certified Travel Company, we provide a wide range of services including holiday packages, hotel bookings, cab services, and group tours.

Our commitment to excellence has helped us serve over 500+ satisfied clients from India and abroad. We believe in creating memorable journeys that combine comfort, adventure, and cultural experiences while maintaining the highest standards of service quality.

Meet Our Founder

Sayan Chatterjee

Founder & CEO | Travel Enthusiast | Social Worker

Sayan Chatterjee, Founder and CEO of Pather Sandhane Tourism (PSTourism), holds a B.Sc. in Physics and an M.Sc. in Biomedical Instrumentation from the University of Calcutta. After working as a teacher in various ICSE schools across India, his passion for travel led him to start vlogging on YouTube.

Driven by the desire to provide affordable, quality travel services, he founded PSTourism, which has since served over 500+ clients from India and other countries. Currently, he also works as an Assistant Branch Post Master with India Post.

Beyond his professional pursuits, Sayan has been actively involved in social work, especially during the COVID-19 pandemic and the aftermath of Cyclone Yaas in the Sundarbans, demonstrating his commitment to community service and social welfare.
