North Bengal Tour Package – 2N/3D

Escape to the serene beauty of Sittong & Kurseong with this perfect offbeat Himalayan getaway for families, couples and small groups.

Package Overview

A perfect offbeat getaway in the Eastern Himalayas

  • Duration: 2 Nights / 3 Days
  • Pick-up/Drop: NJP/Bagdogra
  • Destinations: Sittong, Kurseong + Optional
  • Ideal For: Families, Couples, Small Groups

Escape the chaos of city life and immerse yourself in the lush greenery, scenic tea gardens, and quaint hill villages of North Bengal.

Tour Itinerary

2 Nights / 3 Days Himalayan Escape

1

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer to Sittong

  • Pickup from NJP/Bagdogra
  • Scenic drive to Sittong with sightseeing:
  • Namthing Pokhri - serene high-altitude lake
  • Mahaldiram Tea Estate
  • Ahal Dara Viewpoint
  • Check-in at comfortable homestay
  • Overnight stay at Sittong
2

Day 2: Kurseong Sightseeing + Optional Extension

  • Breakfast at homestay
  • Explore Kurseong's colonial charm:
  • Eagle's Crag Viewpoint
  • Dowhill Eco Park
  • Chimney Strawberries Garden
  • Choose your extension:
  • Tinchuley - orange orchards
  • Tukdah - tea gardens
  • Yogighat - riverside retreat
  • Overnight at selected destination
3

Day 3: Morning Bliss & Return

  • Breakfast at stay
  • Morning exploration:
  • Rhododendron Park
  • Mongpu Rabindra Bhawan
  • Yogighat Hanging Bridge (if applicable)
  • Drive back to NJP/Bagdogra
  • Tour ends with beautiful memories
Note: Itinerary may change based on weather and road conditions.

  • Tranquil environment
