North Bengal Tour Itinerary

3N/4D Perfect Blend of Nature & Culture (NJP-NJP)

1

Day 1: NJP to Sittong

  • Pickup from NJP and transfer to Sittong – The Orange Valley
  • Sightseeing at Namthing Pokhari, Mahaldhiram Tea Garden,Ahaldhara Viewpoint
  • Overnight Stay: Sittong
2

Day 2: Sittong to Darjeeling

  • Travel to Darjeeling – The Queen of Hills
  • Lover’s Meet Point (Teesta & Rangeet Confluence)
    Peasak Tea Garden – Famous for Organic Tea,
    Lamahatta Eco Park – A Must-Visit in Darjeeling
    Tukdah & Tinchuley Viewpoints – Breathtaking Himalayan Views
  • Overnight Stay: Darjeeling
3

Day 3: Darjeeling Sightseeing

  • Tiger Hill sunrise over Kanchenjunga
  • Batasia Loop – The Iconic Toy Train Spot,
    Ghoom Monastery – Oldest Monastery in Darjeeling
    Peace Pagoda & Japanese Temple
    Tenzing Rock & Rangeet Valley Tea Garden,
    Darjeeling Ropeway – The Best View of Tea Estates Himalayan Mountaineering Institute & Darjeeling Zoo
  • Overnight Stay: Darjeeling
4

Day 4: Darjeeling to NJP

  • Transfer to NJP via Mirik sightseeing
  • After breakfast, check out and transfer to NJP via Mirik sightseeing: Mirik Lake – A Serene Boating Experience
    Pashupati Market – Best for Nepalese Handicrafts & Shopping
    Simana Viewpoint – A Stunning Indo-Nepal Border View
    Bokar Monastery – A Spiritual Retreat
    Gopaldhara Viewpoint – North Bengal’s Tea Paradise
  • Tour concludes with unforgettable memories
Note: Sightseeing points are subject to availability based on road conditions and time constraints.
