North Bengal Tour Itinerary

5 Nights / 6 Days Scenic Escape

1

Day 1: Arrival & Transfer to Sittong

  • Pickup from NJP/Bagdogra Airport
  • Scenic drive to Sittong with sightseeing:
  • Namching Pokri, Ahaldhara viewpoints
  • Mahaldhiram tea garden
  • Check-in at deluxe homestay
  • Overnight stay at Sittong
2

Day 2: Explore Kurseong & Surroundings

  • Eagle's Crag viewpoint
  • Dowhill Eco Park
  • Chimney Strawberry Garden
  • Overnight stay at Sittong
3

Day 3: Transfer to Tukdah

  • Drive to quaint Tukdah hill station
  • Mongpu Rabindra Bhawan
  • Yogighat Bridge
  • Tukdah Orchid Garden
  • Rhododendron Park
  • Overnight stay at Tukdah
4

Day 4: Transfer to Darjeeling

  • Lamahata Eco Park
  • Lovers meet point
  • Peasak Tea garden
  • Gumbadara view points
  • Overnight stay at Darjeeling
5

Day 5: Darjeeling Local Sightseeing

  • Tiger Hill sunrise over Kanchenjunga
  • Batasia Loop
  • Ghoom Monastery
  • Peace Pagoda & Japanese Temple
  • Overnight stay at Darjeeling
6

Day 6: Departure

  • Check-out from hotel
  • Transfer to NJP/Bagdogra with sightseeing:
  • Mirik Lake
  • Bokar Monastery
  • Pashupati Market (Nepal Border)
Note: Itinerary may change based on weather and road conditions.
