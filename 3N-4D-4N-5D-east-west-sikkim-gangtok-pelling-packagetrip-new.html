Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan for your 3N/4D Gangtok Trail adventure

1 Pickup from NJP and transfer to Gangtok

Pickup:

NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport (as per booking)

Transfer:

Scenic drive to Gangtok (~4 hrs) through breathtaking mountain landscapes

Evening:

Check-in at hotel; free time to explore the beautiful evening at MG Marg - the vibrant pedestrian street with cafes, shops, and restaurants. Visit Lal Market for local shopping

Overnight:

Stay at Gangtok

Meals:

As per selected plan (CP/MAP/AP)

2 Explore East Sikkim

Morning:

Start early for East Sikkim sightseeing

Tsomgo Lake:

Visit the mesmerizing glacial lake (Changu Lake) at 12,400 ft surrounded by snow-capped peaks. The pristine waters reflect the surrounding mountains

Baba Mandir:

Sacred Baba Harbhajan Singh Temple - shrine dedicated to an Indian Army soldier with captivating folklore

Nathula Pass:

Optional visit to Indo-China border at 14,140 ft (Extra cab cost applicable, subject to permit availability)

Evening:

Return to Gangtok for relaxation

Overnight:

Stay at Gangtok

Meals:

As per selected plan (CP/MAP/AP)

3 Explore Gangtok Local Sightseeing

Full Day:

Explore the best attractions of Gangtok city and surroundings

Banjhakri Falls:

Beautiful waterfall surrounded by lush greenery with an energy park

Hanuman Tok:

Temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman with panoramic views of Gangtok and Kanchenjunga

Bhakthang Falls:

Scenic waterfall enroute to other attractions

Gangtok Ropeway:

Cable car ride offering aerial views of the city (subject to availability)

Other Visits:

Do Drul Chorten (one of the most important stupas), Enchey Monastery, Ganesh Tok (viewpoint with Ganesh temple), Tashi View Point, Flower Exhibition Centre, and Plant Conservation Centre

Evening:

Free time for shopping or relaxation at MG Marg

Overnight:

Stay at Gangtok

Meals:

As per selected plan (CP/MAP/AP)

4 Departure Day

Morning:

After breakfast, check out from hotel

Transfer:

Drive to NJP Railway Station / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your home journey

Memories:

End of trip with wonderful memories of your Gangtok Trail adventure

Meals:

As per selected plan (CP/MAP/AP)
