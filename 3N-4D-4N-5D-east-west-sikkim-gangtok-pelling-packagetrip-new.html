Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan for your East-West Sikkim adventure

1 Arrival – NJP/Bagdogra to Gangtok

Pickup:

NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport (as per booking)

Transfer:

Scenic drive to Gangtok (~4 hrs) through breathtaking mountain landscapes

Evening:

Check-in at Deluxe hotel; free time to explore MG Marg – the vibrant heart of Gangtok with shopping & local cuisine

Meals:

Lunch & Dinner included

2 Gangtok – East Sikkim Sightseeing

Morning:

Start early for Tsomgo Lake (Changu Lake) – a mesmerizing glacial lake at 12,400 ft surrounded by snow-capped peaks

Sightseeing:

Baba Harbhajan Singh Temple – sacred shrine dedicated to an Indian Army soldier with captivating folklore

Optional:

Nathula Pass excursion (additional cost) – Historic Indo-China border at 14,140 ft with spectacular views

Evening:

Return to Gangtok for relaxation

Meals:

Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

3 Gangtok to Pelling – West Sikkim

Transfer:

Morning drive to Pelling (~4 hrs) through scenic West Sikkim with Mt. Kanchenjunga views

Sightseeing:

Pelling Glass Skywalk – Asia's highest glass-bottom skywalk with stunning valley and mountain views

Visit:

Sangachoeling Monastery – one of Sikkim's oldest monasteries, a spiritual haven

Evening:

Check-in at Deluxe hotel and enjoy the peaceful Himalayan surroundings

Meals:

Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner

4 Departure (3N/4D)

Morning:

Wake up to stunning Kanchenjunga sunrise views (weather permitting)

Checkout:

After breakfast, check out from Pelling hotel

Transfer:

Drive to NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport for onward journey

Memories:

End of trip with wonderful memories of East & West Sikkim
