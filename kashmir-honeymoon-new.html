Day 1 : Arrival in Srinagar – The Beginning of Your Love Story

Private Pick-up: From Srinagar Airport

Check-in: At your premium 3-star hotel

Leisure Time: Romantic lakeside stroll or shopping

Stay: Overnight stay in a premium hotel in Srinagar

Day 2 : Srinagar – Sonamarg – Srinagar

Excursion: Full-day trip to Sonamarg (Meadow of Gold)

Sightseeing: Sindh Valley & Thajiwas Glacier

Optional Activities: Horse riding or sledging on snow (extra charges)

Stay: Overnight stay in a premium hotel in Srinagar

Day 3 : Srinagar – Gulmarg – Srinagar

Drive: To Gulmarg (Meadow of Flowers) at 2730m altitude

Sightseeing: Stunning views of Nanga Parbat (weather permitting)

Explore: Asia’s highest ski slopes & the world’s highest golf course

Optional Activities: Gondola ride to Phase 1 & 2 (extra charges)

Stay: Overnight stay in a premium hotel in Srinagar

Day 4 : Srinagar – Pahalgam

En Route Visit: Saffron Fields & Awantipora Ruins (1100-year-old temple)

Arrival: In Pahalgam (Valley of Shepherds) for a scenic retreat

Leisure Time: Bridle walks, river views & photography

Stay: Overnight stay in a premium hotel in Pahalgam

Day 5 : Pahalgam – Srinagar Local Sightseeing

Return: To Srinagar and explore Mughal Gardens (Chashmashahi, Nishat & Shalimar Bagh)

Visit: Shankaracharya Temple

Romantic Shikara Ride: 1-hour private ride on Dal Lake

Explore: Floating markets & Kashmiri handicraft workshops

Stay: Overnight stay in a premium hotel in Srinagar

Day 6 : Srinagar – Doodhpathri – Srinagar

Excursion: Full-day trip to Doodhpathri (Valley of Milk)

Explore: Lush meadows, pine forests & crystal-clear streams

Return: To Srinagar for a houseboat experience

Stay: Overnight stay in a premium houseboat in Srinagar

Day 7 : Departure – Srinagar Airport

Transfer: To Srinagar Airport after breakfast

Farewell: Bid farewell to the enchanting beauty of Kashmir
