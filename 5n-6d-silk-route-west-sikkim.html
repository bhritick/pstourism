Detailed Day-Wise Itinerary

Customizable for solo travelers, couples, families and groups

1 NJP / Bagdogra → Rishikhola / Rorathang

Arrival:

NJP Railway Station / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport

Transfer:

Meet our representative and proceed towards Rishikhola or Rorathang

Explore:

En route sightseeing at Rongpo Market and Rishi River

Stay:

Check into homestay and relax amidst nature. Overnight at Rishikhola / Rorathang

2 Rishikhola → Dzuluk (Old Silk Route)

Drive:

After breakfast, drive towards Dzuluk via the historic Old Silk Route

Visit:

Padamchen Village and scenic Que Khola Waterfall

Afternoon:

Arrive at Dzuluk, check into homestay. Enjoy lunch and peaceful afternoon with panoramic mountain views

Stay:

Overnight at Dzuluk

3 Dzuluk → Gangtok via Nathula Corridor

Morning:

Early sunrise from Dzuluk View Point (Mt. Kanchenjunga view on clear days)

Drive:

After breakfast, proceed through the iconic Zigzag Road of Dzuluk

Visit:

Kupup Lake, Elephant Lake, Baba Harbhajan Mandir, Nathula Pass (subject to permit & extra cab cost), Tsomgo (Changu) Lake

Evening:

Arrive at Gangtok and enjoy evening leisure at MG Marg. Overnight at Gangtok

4 Gangtok → Pelling (West Sikkim)

Transfer:

After breakfast, transfer to Pelling, the gateway to West Sikkim

En Route:

Visit Bird Park and historic Rabdentse Ruins

Views:

Enjoy stunning views of Kanchenjunga range

Stay:

Check into hotel and relax amidst tranquil Himalayan surroundings. Overnight at Pelling

5 Pelling Local Sightseeing

Full Day:

Exploring Pelling and West Sikkim attractions

Visit:

Rimbi River & Orange Garden, Khecheopalri Lake (sacred wish-fulfilling lake), Darap Village, Kanchenjunga Waterfall, Sangacholing Monastery & Chenrezig Statue, Glass Sky Walk (Pelling Skywalk)

Stay:

Overnight at Pelling

6 Pelling → Departure

Morning:

After breakfast, check out from hotel

Transfer:

Proceed for return journey to NJP / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport

End:

Tour ends with unforgettable memories of Silk Route & West Sikkim
