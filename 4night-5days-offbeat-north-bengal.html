Best Time to Visit North Bengal

North Bengal offers distinct experiences throughout the year:

Spring (March-May)

Ideal for pleasant weather and blooming flowers. Perfect for nature walks and sightseeing.

Monsoon (June-September)

Lush green landscapes with occasional rain showers. Best for photography enthusiasts.

Autumn (October-November)

Crystal clear views of the Himalayas. Great for mountain views and outdoor activities.

Winter (December-February)

Chilly weather with possible snowfall in higher areas. Perfect for cozy homestay experiences.
