Detailed Itinerary

Day-by-day plan across Sittong, Kurseong & Tukdah

1 Arrival at NJP / IXB / Siliguri

Pickup:

Driver receives you at NJP/IXB/Siliguri and transfers to Sittong with scenic Teesta views.

Explore:

Namching Pokri, Mahaldiram Tea Estate, Ahaldhara Viewpoint (en route)

Stay:

Overnight stay at Sittong

2 Explore Kurseong

Sightseeing:

Eagle’s Crag, Dowhill Eco Park, Chimney Strawberries Garden & Park (IOF), etc.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Sittong

3 Sittong → Tukdah with Sightseeing

Explore:

Tukdah Orchid Garden, Rhododendron Park, Mongpu Rabindra Bhawan, Yogighat Bridge, etc.

Stay:

Overnight stay at Tukdah

4 Tukdah Sightseeing

Explore:

Lamahatta Eco Park, Gumbadara View Points, Lover’s Meet Point, Peasak Tea Garden, etc.

Stay:

Overnight stay (as per plan)

5 Departure

Transfer:

Transfer to NJP for your onward journey
