Suggested Itinerary

Customizable for couples, families and groups

1 Pickup NJP/Bagdogra → Ravangla

  • Warm welcome at NJP Railway Station or Bagdogra Airport.
  • Private transfer to Ravangla; check-in at hotel.
  • Evening free; acclimatize and enjoy serene surroundings.
  • Overnight stay in Ravangla.

2 Ravangla Sightseeing

  • Visit Buddha Park (Tathagata Tsal) and Ralong Monastery.
  • Titanic View Point and local viewpoints (weather permitting).
  • Explore local markets and cafe scenes.
  • Overnight stay in Ravangla.

3 Ravangla → Gangtok via Namchi

  • Drive through Temi Tea Garden.
  • Stop at Samdruptse Hill and Char Dham (Namchi).
  • Arrive Gangtok, hotel check-in; evening leisure at MG Marg.
  • Overnight stay in Gangtok.

4 Gangtok Local & Departure

  • Gangtok local points: Tashi View Point, Ganesh Tok, Enchey/Do Drul Chorten (time permitting).
  • Flower Exhibition Centre and Handicraft Centre (optional).
  • Transfer to NJP/Bagdogra for onward journey.
