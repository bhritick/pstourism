Introduction

North Sikkim is a paradise for nature lovers, adventure seekers, and spiritual travelers. Known for its breathtaking landscapes, snow-clad mountains, lush green valleys, and serene monasteries, this region offers an unforgettable experience. If you are planning a trip, here is a detailed guide on the best North Sikkim tourist places that you must visit.

Gurudongmar Lake

Altitude: 17,800 feet

Best Time to Visit: April to June, October to December

Highlights: One of the highest lakes in the world, sacred to Buddhists, Hindus, and Sikhs.

Why Visit? Offers panoramic views, crystal-clear water, and an unforgettable high-altitude experience.

Yumthang Valley

Altitude: 11,693 feet

Best Time to Visit: March to June (for flowers), October to December (for snow)

Highlights: Rhododendron blooms, hot springs, and stunning landscapes.

Why Visit? Known as "Switzerland of the East" with breathtaking floral beauty.

Lachen

Altitude: 8,838 feet

Best Time to Visit: October to June

Highlights: Gateway to Gurudongmar Lake and Chopta Valley, stunning mountain views.

Why Visit? Perfect for nature lovers and a gateway to North Sikkim’s hidden gems.

Lachung

Altitude: 8,610 feet

Best Time to Visit: October to May

Highlights: Scenic waterfalls, Buddhist monasteries, and proximity to Yumthang Valley.

Why Visit? Ideal for those seeking tranquility and cultural experiences.

Zero Point

Altitude: 15,300 feet

Best Time to Visit: October to May

Highlights: Snow-covered landscapes, breathtaking views.

Why Visit? Experience untouched beauty and high-altitude adventure.

Chopta Valley

Altitude: 13,200 feet

Best Time to Visit: March to June, October to December

Highlights: Snow-covered mountains, rhododendron forests, and trekking opportunities.

Why Visit? Ideal for nature lovers, adventure seekers, and photographers.

How to Reach North Sikkim

By Air

The nearest airport is Bagdogra Airport (IXB), located 125 km from Gangtok. From Bagdogra, hire a cab or take a shared vehicle to Gangtok, then proceed to North Sikkim.

By Train

The nearest railway station is New Jalpaiguri (NJP). From NJP, take a taxi or bus to Gangtok and then travel to North Sikkim.

By Road

Gangtok serves as the gateway to North Sikkim. You can hire private taxis or join group tours for a comfortable journey.

Travel Tips for North Sikkim

  • Permit Required: North Sikkim is a restricted area, and an Inner Line Permit (ILP) is mandatory for travelers.
  • Pack Wisely: Carry warm clothes, sturdy shoes, and essential medicines.
  • Altitude Sickness: Acclimatize properly before visiting high-altitude areas like Gurudongmar Lake.
  • Network Connectivity: Limited mobile network, BSNL and Airtel work best.
  • Book in Advance: Secure your stay and permits early, especially during peak season.
