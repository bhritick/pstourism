Detailed Itinerary

Day-wise breakdown of your Silk Route Trail adventure

1 NJP/Siliguri/Bagdogra to Rishikhola/Rorathang

Pickup:

Our representative will meet you at NJP Railway Station / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport (IXB)

Transfer:

Drive to Rishikhola / Rorathang – gateway to the Old Silk Route (approx. 4-5 hours)

Sightseeing:

En-route visit Sevak Kalibari Temple, iconic Coronation Bridge, scenic Rishi River / Rongpo River viewpoints

Checkin:

Arrive at Rishikhola/Rorathang and check-in to your hotel

Evening:

Rest and acclimatize to the mountain environment, enjoy peaceful village atmosphere

Overnight:

Comfortable stay at Rishikhola / Rorathang

Meals:

Dinner as per selected meal plan

2 Rishikhola to Zuluk/Padamchen/Lungthung

Morning:

After breakfast, check out and drive towards Zuluk region

Transfer:

Scenic drive to Zuluk / Padamchen / Lungthung through winding mountain roads

Sightseeing:

Visit Rongoli Market – experience local Sikkimese culture and commerce

Que Khola Falls:

Stop at the beautiful Que Khola waterfall for photos and refreshment

Zigzag Road:

Experience the famous Zuluk zigzag road with 32 spectacular hairpin bends – perfect for photography

Checkin:

Arrive and check-in to your homestay/hotel at Zuluk / Padamchen / Lungthung

Evening:

Enjoy stunning sunset views over the Eastern Himalayas

Overnight:

Stay at Zuluk / Padamchen / Lungthung

Meals:

Breakfast & Dinner as per selected meal plan

3 Zuluk to Gangtok via Nathang Valley

Early Morning:

After breakfast, check out and begin your scenic drive towards Gangtok

Thambi/Zuluk View Point:

First stop at Thambi View Point or Zuluk View Point for spectacular panoramic views of Mt. Kanchenjunga

Nathang Valley:

Visit the stunning high-altitude Nathang Valley at 13,500 ft – one of the most scenic spots in Sikkim

Elephant Lake:

Stop at the sacred Elephant Lake (Tsomgo alternative) surrounded by snow-capped mountains

Kupuk Lake:

Visit the pristine Kupuk Lake, a hidden gem of the Silk Route

Baba Mandir:

Pay respects at both Old Baba Mandir and New Baba Mandir – dedicated to Baba Harbhajan Singh, a revered Army soldier

Nathula Pass (Optional):

Optional visit to Nathula Pass (Indo-China border) at 14,140 ft – requires extra cab cost and advance permit booking

Transfer:

Continue drive to Gangtok via scenic mountain roads

Checkin:

Arrive at Gangtok and check-in to your hotel

Evening:

Leisure time to explore MG Marg or rest after the day's journey

Overnight:

Comfortable stay at Gangtok

Meals:

Breakfast & Dinner as per selected meal plan

4 Departure (with Optional Gangtok Local Sightseeing)

Morning:

After breakfast, check out from hotel

Optional Sightseeing:

Gangtok local sightseeing can be arranged (subject to time and preference): Tashi View Point, Ganesh Tok, Hanuman Tok, Do Drul Chorten, Enchey Monastery, Banjhakri Falls, Flower Exhibition Centre

Transfer:

Drive back to NJP Railway Station / Siliguri / Bagdogra Airport (IXB) for your onward journey

Departure:

Tour concludes with wonderful memories of the Old Silk Route Trail

Meals:

Breakfast as per selected meal plan

Note:

We recommend booking train/flight after 6:00 PM from NJP/Bagdogra to avoid any rush

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common queries about the Silk Route Trail package

The package includes: Quality accommodation at Rishikhola/Rorathang, Zuluk/Padamchen/Lungthung, and Gangtok (Standard/Deluxe/Premium options available), meals as per your selected plan (CP/MAP/AP), personal cab with experienced driver-cum-guide for entire journey, all necessary Sikkim permits including Protected Area Permit for Silk Route region, toll taxes, parking fees, and dedicated client support team. Please note that Nathula Pass permit and extra cab cost, entry fees, and personal expenses are not included.

Nathula Pass is optional: The visit to Nathula Pass (Indo-China border at 14,140 ft) requires extra cab cost and is subject to permit availability and weather conditions. The permit needs to be arranged in advance with valid ID proofs. You can request this addition when booking. Nathula Pass remains closed on Mondays and Tuesdays and may close unexpectedly due to weather or security reasons.

Best seasons for Silk Route: April to June (spring/summer) offers pleasant weather with blooming rhododendrons, clear sky views, and comfortable temperatures. September to November (autumn) provides crisp air, excellent visibility of Kanchenjunga, and vibrant landscapes. December to February (winter) offers stunning snow-covered vistas but can be extremely cold with temperatures dropping below freezing – warm clothing essential. Avoid monsoon season (July-August) due to heavy rainfall and landslide risks on mountain roads.
